**I. Dhaka Division Overview**

*   **Formation:** 1829
*   **Status:** Contains the **Capital City (Dhaka)** of Bangladesh.
*   **Size:** ~20,508 sq km.
*   **Population:** **Most populous division** (>44 million), fastest growing (1.94% rate).
*   **Districts:** **13** (Including Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, Tangail, Kishoreganj, Faridpur etc.)
*   **Location:** Central Bangladesh, bordered by most other divisions.
*   **Key Rivers:** Buriganga, Dhaleswari.

**II. Dhaka City**

*   **Status:** Capital of Bangladesh, Dhaka Division, and Dhaka District.
*   **Size:** **Largest city** in Bangladesh, among top 10 most populous globally.
*   **Population:** Extremely high density.
    *   Greater Dhaka (2024 est.): ~23.9 million
    *   City Proper (2022): ~10.3 million
*   **Nickname:** **"Rickshaw Capital of the World"**.
*   **Commercial Hubs:** Motijheel, Gulshan, Karwan Bazar.
*   **Industrial Areas:** Tejgaon, Hazaribagh.

**III. Administrative Structure (Dhaka City & Division)**

*   **City Corporations (within Dhaka Division):**
    *   **Dhaka North City Corporation (DNCC)**
    *   **Dhaka South City Corporation (DSCC)** (Split from original Dhaka City Corp in 2011)
    *   Gazipur City Corporation
    *   Narayanganj City Corporation
*   **Mayors:** Elected for 5-year terms for each City Corporation.

**IV. History**

*   **Ancient Roots:** Pre-Muslim existence, part of Bikrampur/Sena Dynasty, later Sonargaon hub.
*   **Mughal Era:**
    *   Became **Capital of Mughal Bengal in 1608**.
    *   Administrator: **Islam Khan Chishti**.
    *   Named **Jahangirnagar** (after Emperor Jahangir).
*   **Etymology (Origin of "Dhaka"):** Uncertain. Theories include:
    *   *Dhak* tree
    *   *Dhakeshwari* (Goddess)
    *   *Dhak* (drum instrument played by Islam Khan)
    *   *Dhakka* (watchtower)

**V. Demographics (Dhaka Division)**

*   **Population:** ~44.2 million
*   **Religion:** Predominantly **Muslim (~93.4%)**, followed by Hindu (~6.3%).

**VI. Education**

*   **Highest concentration** of educational institutions in Bangladesh.
*   **Universities (Division):** ~17 Public, ~67 Private.
*   **Key Institutions in Dhaka City:**
    *   **University of Dhaka (DU):** Est. 1921 (Oldest University in the region).
    *   **Dhaka College:** Est. 1841 (Oldest institution for higher education in the city).
    *   Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET).
    *   Dhaka Medical College (DMC).
    *   Jahangirnagar University (JU) (Savar, near Dhaka).
    *   Jagannath University (JnU).
    *   Many prominent schools/colleges (Notre Dame, Viqarunnisa, Ideal, etc.).

**VII. Important Places & Landmarks**

*   **National Symbols:**
    *   National Parliament House (Jatiya Sangsad Bhaban)
    *   National Martyrs' Monument (Jatiyo Smriti Shoudho - Savar)
    *   Shaheed Minar (Language Movement Memorial)
    *   Martyred Intellectuals Memorial (Rayer Bazar)
*   **Historical Sites:**
    *   **Lalbagh Fort** (Mughal Fort)
    *   **Ahsan Manzil** ("Pink Palace" - Nawab Residence, now museum)
    *   **Panam Nagar** (Sonargaon - Old merchant city ruins)
    *   Sonargaon (Ancient Capital of Bengal)
    *   Baliati Palace (Manikganj)
*   **Religious Sites:**
    *   Baitul Mukarram (National Mosque)
    *   Dhakeshwari Temple (National Temple)
    *   St. Mary's Cathedral
    *   International Buddhist Monastery

**VIII. Culture & Food**

*   **Festival:** **Shakrain** - Kite flying festival in Old Dhaka (end of Poush).
*   **Food Specialties:**
    *   **Bakarkhani** (Traditional layered bread)
    *   **Biryani / Kacchi Biryani**
    *   Halim
    *   Street Food (Very popular and diverse)

**IX. Transport**

*   Dominated by **Rickshaws**.
*   CNG Auto-rickshaws.
*   Public Buses (BRTC & Private).
*   **Sadarghat River Port:** Key terminal on the Buriganga River.

**X. Renowned Personalities (Associated with Dhaka)**

*   **Fazlur Rahman Khan:** Structural Engineer ("Einstein of structural engineering", designed Sears/Willis Tower frame).
*   **Sir Khwaja Ahsanullah:** Nawab of Dhaka, Philanthropist.
*   Sabina Yasmin: Singer.
*   Azam Khan: Singer ("Rock Guru").
*   Bulbul Ahmed: Actor/Director.

**XI. Major Problems / Disadvantages of Living in Dhaka City**

*   **Traffic Congestion:** Severe, time-consuming.
*   **Pollution:** Extremely high (Air, water, noise). Ranked among worst globally.
*   **Overpopulation / High Density:** Leads to strain on resources.
*   Slum Dwelling / Urban Poverty.
*   Crime Rates.
*   Food Adulteration.
