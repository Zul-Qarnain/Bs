## IV. Rajshahi Division

### Notes per Slide

*   **Location & Demography (Slide 2):**
    *   Area: **17,974.68 sq km**.
    *   Population (Total): **18,355,554** (2011 data, though slide says 2022 it lists 2011 numbers in tables sometimes).
    *   Main rivers: Padma, Jamuna, Atrai, Mahananda; Chalan Beel (notable wetland).
*   **Administration (Slides 3-4):**
    *   Rajshahi Town established: **18th century**.
    *   Rajshahi Municipality formed: **1876**, City Corporation: **1991**.
    *   Rangpur separated from Rajshahi Division to form a new Division on **25 January 2010**.
    *   Comprises **8 Districts** and **66 Upazilas**.
    *   **Bogra** is the most populous district (**3.4 million**) and has the highest density (**1173/km²**).
    *   **Joypurhat** has the highest literacy rate (**57.5%**).
    *   **Sirajganj** has the lowest literacy rate (**42.1%**).
*   **Literacy Rate & Educational Institutions (Slides 5-6):**
    *   Average literacy: **48.0%**.
    *   Includes 3 universities (Rajshahi University 1953, RUET 2003, PSTU 2008) and 2 medical colleges.
    *   Notable colleges like Rajshahi College (1873) and schools like Rajshahi Collegiate School (1828).
*   **Main Sources of Income (Slide 7):**
    *   Agriculture dominates (**59.35%**).
    *   Commerce (**14.25%**) and service (**8.97%**) are significant.
*   **Tourist Spots (Slides 9-18):**
    *   **The Padma River:** Offers scenic views, boat rides.
    *   **Varendra Research Museum:** Located in Rajshahi University campus, **oldest museum in Bangladesh**.
    *   **Puthia Temple Complex:** Near Rajshahi, 17th-century Hindu temples (Govinda, Shiva, Jagannath) with terracotta sculptures.
    *   **Somapura Mohabihar (Paharpur):** UNESCO World Heritage site, important 8th-century Buddhist monastery.
    *   **Bagha Mosque:** Early 16th-century Sultanate mosque, known for terracotta decorations.
    *   **Varendra Park:** Recreational spot in Rajshahi city.
    *   **Dighapatia Rajbari:** Historic palace, former feudal lords' residence.
    *   **Chapai Nawabganj:** Known as the "mango hub"; contains ancient ruins of Gauda (7th C city) with sites like Darasbari Mosque (built 1504 by Shamsuddin Yusuf Shah).
*   **Food (Slide 19):**
    *   Kalo Bhuna (spicy goat curry), Chomchom (syrupy sweet, Rajshahi specialty), Bhuna Khichuri, Kalai Ruti (flatbread), Begun Bharta (eggplant mash), Nihari (meat stew), Jhal Muri.
*   **Rajshahi Silk (Slide 20):**
    *   High-quality fabric, especially for saris.
    *   Received **Geographical Indication (GI) status in 2021**.
    *   Silk production in Indian subcontinent before **13th century**.
    *   "Bengal Silk" was a major trade item (17th-19th centuries).
*   **Renowned Personalities (Slides 22-26):**
    *   **Rani Bhabani (1716–1803):** "Natorer Rani", Hindu zamindar, managed largest zamindari (**~32,970 km²**). Tried social reform (widow remarriage). Helped during **Great Bengal famine of 1770**.
    *   **Radhagobinda Basak (1885-1982):** Bengali scholar, translated Kautilya's Arthashastra. Received highest Presidential honor from Indian Govt. in **1965**.
    *   **Maharani Sharatsundari Devi:** Benevolent zamindar from Puthia.
    *   **Hemantakumari:** Inherited zamindari, known for charitable deeds, donated for student hostel, Sanskrit college, water works in Rajshahi.
    *   **Saratkumar Ray (1876-1946):** Co-founder of Varendra Research Society and Varendra Research Museum.
