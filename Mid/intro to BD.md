Okay, here are the key points from your Bangladesh presentation, formatted for easy memorization for a quiz.

**I. Introduction & Location**

- **Official Name:** People’s Republic of Bangladesh
    
- **Location:** Southern Asia, NE Indian subcontinent.
    
- **Borders:**
    
    - West, North, East: **India** (States: West Bengal, Assam, Meghalaya, Tripura)
        
    - Southeast: **Myanmar** (Burma)
        
    - South: **Bay of Bengal**
        
- **Area:** 148,460 sq. km
    
- **Population (2024 est.):** ~173.5 million (Very high density)
    
- **Geography:** Significant delta region, low sea level, receives Himalayan water.
    

**II. History**

- Historically part of **Bengal**.
    
- **1947-1971:** Province of Pakistan (Initially **East Bengal**, renamed **East Pakistan** in 1955).
    
- **Independence Declared:** **March 26, 1971** (National Day)
    
- **Victory Day / Independence Secured:** **December 16, 1971** (Surrender of Pakistani troops to joint Bangladeshi/Indian forces).
    
- **"Bangladesh" Meaning:** Land of the Bengalis.
    

**III. Administrative Structure (Hierarchy & Numbers)**

- **Mnemonic:** **D**ivisions **D**o **U**nderstand **C**ity/**M**unicipality/**U**nion (Divisions -> Districts -> Upazilas -> City Corp / Municipal Corp / Union Parishad)

**IV. Climate**

- **Type:** Subtropical Monsoon
    
- **Key Features:** Seasonal rain, warm temps, high humidity.
    
- **Three Seasons:**
    
    1. Hot, Humid Summer (March - June)
        
    2. Rainy Monsoon (June - October)
        
    3. Cool, Dry Winter (October - March)
        
- **Warmest Month:** April (Max 32-40°C)
    
- **Coldest Month:** January (Avg ~10°C)
    
- **Natural Calamities:** Floods, Cyclones, Tornadoes, Tidal Bores (Frequent).
    

**V. Demographics**

- **Density:** Most densely populated large country in the world.
    
- **Ethnicity:** **98% Bengali**, rest are tribal groups (Chakma, Marma, Garo, Santal etc.) mostly in Chittagong Hill Tracts & Northern areas.
    
- **Language:** **Bangla (Bengali)** - Official. English used in official tasks/higher education. Urdu spoken by small minority.
    
- **Religion:** **~89% Muslim**, ~10% Hindu, small numbers of Buddhists, Christians, Animists.
    
- **Population Rank:** 8th most populous country.
    
- **Growth Rate:** High (1.22% approx), govt promoting birth control.
    
- **Health Success:** Nationwide childhood immunization.
    
- **Health Challenge:** Water-borne diseases (due to living on floodplains).
    
- **Key Stats (Approx. / Recent):**
    
    - Total Population: ~174.7 million
        
    - Fertility Rate: ~1.9 - 2.3 births per woman
        
    - Maternal Mortality: ~123 per 100,000
        
    - Child Mortality (Under 1): ~25 per 1,000
        
    - Under-Five Mortality: ~29 per 1,000
        
    - Life Expectancy: ~73 years
        

**VI. Economy**

- **1971:** Started extremely poor.
    
- **Recent Growth:** ~6% (2023), down from ~7.1% (2022).
    
- **Poverty Reduction:** Significant progress.
    
    - National Poverty Line: Dropped to **18.7%** (2022) from 31.5% (2010).
        
    - Extreme Poverty (Lower Line): ~11.3% (2018).
        
- **Income Status:** Reached **Lower Middle-Income Country** status in 2015.
    
- **LDC Graduation:** Met criteria in 2018, on track for graduation from Least Developed Country list in **2024**.
    

**VII. Education**

- **System:** Highly subsidized by Govt. Primary -> Secondary -> Higher Secondary -> University.
    
- **Oversight:** **9 Regional Education Boards**.
    
- **Key Exams:** SSC (Secondary), HSC (Higher Secondary).
    
- **Female Education:** Free up to Higher Secondary, stipends available.
    
- **Diversity:** General, Madrasah, English Medium/Version systems exist.
    
- **Universities:** ~56 Public, ~115 Private.
    

**VIII. Culture**

- **Nature:** Mixed culture, "melting pot".
    
- **Influences:** Hinduism, Buddhism, **Islam** (most pervasive).
    
- **Key Festivals:**
    
    - Muslim: Eid-ul-Fitr, Eid-ul-Azha
        
    - Hindu: Durga Puja
        
    - Christian: Christmas (Baradin)
        
    - **Common/Secular:** **Pahela Baishakh** (Bangla New Year) - Very Important!
        
    - **National:** Independence Day (Mar 26), Language Day (Feb 21), Victory Day (Dec 16).
        
- **Literature:**
    
    - Oldest Text: **Charyapada** (8th C)
        
    - Key Figures: **Rabindranath Tagore** (Nobel Laureate), **Kazi Nazrul Islam**.
        
- **Painting:**
    
    - Pioneer: **Zainul Abedin** (Famous for 1943 famine sketches).
        
- **Performing Arts:**
    
    - **Drama:** Old tradition, popular theatre groups, Baily Road ("Natak Para").
        
    - **Dance:** Classical, Folk, Tribal (Monipuri, Santal).
        
    - **Jatra:** Folk drama (mythology/heroism), vital rural entertainment historically.
        
- **Traditional Transport:** Bullock/buffalo carts, tomtoms (horse carts), Palki (weddings).
