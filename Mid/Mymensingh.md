**I. Quick Facts & Overview**

*   **Status:** One of the 8 administrative divisions of Bangladesh.
*   **Formation:** **2015** (Newest Division at the time of creation, carved from northern Dhaka Div).
*   **Area:** ~10,485 sq km.
*   **Population:** ~12.2 million (2022 Census).
*   **Headquarters:** Mymensingh City.
*   **Significance:** Major financial and **education hub** of north-central Bangladesh.
*   **Location:** North-central Bangladesh.
    *   North: India (Meghalaya state)
    *   South: Dhaka Division
    *   East: Sylhet Division
    *   West: Rangpur & Rajshahi Divisions
*   **Key Rivers:** Old Brahmaputra, Someshwari, Kangsha, Bhogai.

**II. Administration**

*   **Districts:** **4**
    *   **Mnemonic:** **MJNS** (**M**ymensingh, **J**amalpur, **N**etrokona, **S**herpur)
*   **Upazilas:** Multiple under each district (specific numbers less crucial than the 4 districts).

**III. History & Naming**

*   **Origin of Name:** Anglization of "Momen Singh" (Muslim saint/ruler).
*   **Previous Name:** Nasirabad / Nasratshahi.
*   **British Era:** Greater Mymensingh District created 1787 (originally included Kishoreganj & Tangail, which are now in Dhaka Div).
*   **Division Official Announcement:** September 14, 2015.

**IV. Mymensingh City & District**

*   **Mymensingh City:** Capital of Division/District, on Brahmaputra River, ~120 km N of Dhaka. 8th largest city by population (~577k).
*   **Mymensingh District:** Most populous district in the division.

**V. Education Hub ("Educational Capital")**

*   **Reputation:** Known as a major center for education.
*   **Key Institutions:**
    *   **Bangladesh Agricultural University (BAU):** Premier agricultural university (est. 1961), large campus.
    *   **Jatiya Kabi Kazi Nazrul Islam University (JKKNIU):** Public university in Trishal (est. 2006), arts focus.
    *   **Mymensingh Medical College:** Major medical institution.
    *   **Ananda Mohan College:** Old, prestigious public college (est. 1908 as college).
    *   **Mymensingh Zilla School:** Very old, prestigious boys' school (est. 1853). First Headmaster: Bhagwan Chandra Bose (father of J.C. Bose).
    *   **Vidyamoyee Govt. Girls' High School:** Reputed girls' school.
    *   **Mymensingh Girls' Cadet College:** **First** girls' cadet college in Bangladesh (est. 1982).
    *   **Women Teachers' Training College:** **Only** one in Bangladesh, housed in Shashi Lodge.
    *   **National Academy for Primary Education (NAPE):** Important training institute.
    *   Other Research Institutes: BINA (Nuclear Agriculture), Fisheries Research, Veterinary Training.

**VI. Tourist & Historical Places**

*   **Palaces/Buildings:**
    *   **Shashi Lodge:** Zamindar palace (Maharaja Shashikant Acharya), rebuilt 1905-1911, colonial style, used for Women Teachers' Training College/Museum.
    *   **Muktagacha Zamindar House:** 18th C, traditional Bengali architecture. Origin of Muktagacha name linked to Muktaram Kormokar + Gachha (lampstand).
    *   **Alexander Castle:** Built 1889 by Maharaja Suryakanta Acharya Chowdhury, European style, known as "Iron Kuthi", now Teacher Training College Library.
    *   Gouripur Rajbari.
*   **Museums:**
    *   **Shilpacharya Zainul Abedin Sangrahashala:** Art museum dedicated to Zainul Abedin (est. 1975).
*   **Nature/Scenic Spots:**
    *   **Birishiri (Netrokona):** Scenic area, **Ceramic Hills**, **Blue Water Lake**, Shomeshwari River, Garo hills.
    *   Durgapur Someswari River (Netrokona).
    *   Madhutila Ecopark (Sherpur).
    *   Botanical Garden (at BAU).
    *   Haor areas (nearby, e.g., Kishoreganj - note Kishoreganj is NOT in Mymensingh Division).
    *   Kangsa River (Netrokona).

**VII. Culture**

*   **Mymensingh Geetika:** Famous collection of folk ballads/songs (e.g., *Mahua*, *Malua*, *Chandrabati*). Collected/edited by **Dinesh Chandra Sen** (pub. 1923).
*   **Food Specialties:**
    *   **Muktagachar Monda:** *Signature sweet*, originated 1824 by Ram Gopal Pal.
    *   Balisher Mishti / Chamcham (Netrokona).
    *   Chepa Shutki (fermented fish).
*   **Tribes:** Primarily **Garo** people (call themselves 'Mandi'), especially in Durgapur, Haluaghat, Sherpur areas. Also Santals, etc.

**VIII. Renowned Personalities (Associated with the Region)**

*   **Sir Jagadish Chandra Bose:** Scientist (Father was Headmaster of Mymensingh Zilla School).
*   **Zainul Abedin:** Artist ("Shilpacharya").
*   Upendrakishore Ray Chowdhury: Writer/Painter (Born in Mymensingh region).
*   Helal Hafiz: Poet.

**IX. Key Problems**

*   Falling behind economically despite potential.
*   Inadequate infrastructure (roads, drainage).
*   Traffic congestion (esp. unregulated auto-rickshaws).
