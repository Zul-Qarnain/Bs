**I. Overview & Geography**

*   **Name:** Chattogram Division (Official), Chittagong Division (Common)
*   **Size:** **Largest** administrative division in Bangladesh by area (~34,000 sq km).
*   **Location:** **Southeasternmost** part of Bangladesh.
*   **Boundaries:**
    *   North: Dhaka & Sylhet Divisions
    *   South: Bay of Bengal & Myanmar (Arakan)
    *   East: India (Mizoram, Tripura, Chin states) & Myanmar
    *   West: Dhaka & Barisal Divisions
*   **Includes:** Mainland districts + **Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT)**.
*   **Key Geographical Features:**
    *   Hilly regions (esp. in CHT: Rangamati, Khagrachhari, Bandarban)
    *   Forests
    *   **Cox's Bazar:** World's **longest natural unbroken sea beach** (120 km).
    *   **St. Martin's Island:** Bangladesh's **only coral reef**.
    *   Karnaphuli River (Chittagong City located on its banks)
*   **Significance:** Important agriculture, hydroelectric power (Kaptai), **major seaport (Chittagong)**, vital commercial/industrial hub.

**II. History (Chittagong City/Region)**

*   Ancient port (history since 4th C BC), Neolithic inhabitation.
*   Arab traders from 9th C AD.
*   Conquered by Sultan Fakruddin Mubarak Shah (1340).
*   Portuguese presence (16th C), called it **Porto Grande de Bengala**.
*   Mughal conquest (1666), renamed **Islamabad**.
*   Suffered losses during the 1971 Liberation War.
*   Official name changed from Chittagong to **Chattogram** in 2018.

**III. Administration**

*   **Mnemonic for CHT Districts:** **RBK** (**R**angamati, **B**andarban, **K**hagrachhari)
*   **Districts:** **11** (Chittagong, Cox's Bazar, **R**angamati, **B**andarban, **K**hagrachhari, Feni, Lakshmipur, Comilla, Noakhali, Brahmanbaria, Chandpur)
*   **Sub-districts (Upazilas):** ~99-104
*   **City Corporation:** 1 (Chattogram City Corporation - formed 1990)
*   **Division Formed:** 1984 (Sylhet separated in 1995).

**IV. Demographics**

*   **Population:** ~33.2 million (2022 Census), largest division by population too.
*   **Density:** ~979 people/sq km (High).
*   **Urban Population:** ~34% (Higher than national average).
*   **Religion (Approx. 2022):**
    *   Muslim: ~90%
    *   Hindu: ~6.6%
    *   Buddhist: ~2.9% (Higher due to CHT population)
    *   Christian: ~0.2%
*   **Literacy:** ~53% (Census 2022)

**V. Chittagong City & Port**

*   **Rank:** **2nd largest city** in Bangladesh.
*   **Significance:** "Business Capital" of Bangladesh.
*   **Port:** **Busiest port** in Bangladesh and the Bay of Bengal. Managed by Chittagong Port Authority (CPA). Located on Karnaphuli River.
*   **EPZ:** Site of Bangladesh's **first Export Processing Zone** (1983).

**VI. Chittagong Hill Tracts (CHT)**

*   **Districts:** **Rangamati, Bandarban, Khagrachhari (RBK)**.
*   **Geography:** Extensively hilly area (~13,295 sq km).
*   **People:** Home to diverse **Indigenous (Adivasi) communities** (distinct from majority Bengalis, Mongoloid origin).
    *   **Chakma:** Largest group, Buddhist, leader is Chakma Raja, Jhum cultivation.
    *   **Marma:** Second largest, Burmese ancestry.
    *   **Tripura:** Significant group.
    *   Others: Tanchangya, Mru, Bawm, etc.
*   **Culture:** Distinct languages, dress (e.g., Pinon & Khadi for Chakma women), customs.
*   **Major Festival:** **Boisabi** (celebrates New Year in mid-April).
    *   Name combines: **Boi**suk (Tripura), **Sa**ngrai (Marma), **Bi**ju (Chakma/Tanchangya).
    *   Rituals include floating flowers (Phul Biju), feasts (Pachon curry).

**VII. Key Educational Institutions**

*   **University of Chittagong (CU):** Public, multidisciplinary, **largest campus** in BD.
*   **Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET):** Public engineering uni.
*   **Others:** Chattogram Medical University, CVASU (Veterinary), Cumilla University, NSTU (Noakhali), RSTU (Rangamati).

**VIII. Important Places / Tourism Hotspots**

*   **Beaches:** **Cox's Bazar**, Patenga, Inani, Guliakhali.
*   **Hills/Valleys:** **Sajek Valley** ("Queen of Hills"), Nilachol (Bandarban), Lalmai Hills (Comilla).
*   **Lakes:** Foy's Lake (man-made), Kaptai Lake (hydroelectric reservoir), Boga Lake (natural, high altitude).
*   **Islands:** **St. Martin's** (coral), Nijhum Dwip.
*   **Historical/Cultural:**
    *   Chittagong War Cemetery (WWII)
    *   Ethnological Museum (Agrabad, only one in BD)
    *   Shalban Bihar & Mainimati Ruins (Buddhist archaeological sites, Comilla)
    *   Golden Temple (Bandarban)
*   **Parks/Nature:** Kaptai National Park, Dulahazara Safari Park, Himchari National Park.
*   **Other:** Marine Drive (Cox's Bazar), Hanging Bridge (Rangamati).

**IX. Culture & Food**

*   **Mezban/Mejbani:** **Signature traditional feast** of Chittagong, esp. beef curry (Mejbani Gosht). Social event for various occasions.
*   **Kala Bhuna:** Famous spicy dark beef curry.
*   **Shutki:** Dried fish, very popular ingredient.
*   **Seafood:** Abundant and famous.
*   **Bamboo Chicken/Fish:** CHT specialty.
*   **Boisabi:** Major CHT cultural festival (see CHT section).

**X. Notable Personalities**

*   **Literature:** Daulat Kazi (Medieval Poet), **Syed Waliullah** (Novelist - *Lalsalu*).
*   **Independence/History:** **Pritilota Waddedar**, Munir Chowdhury, Bir Sreshtho Ruhul Amin, Sargent Zahurul Haque.
*   **Arts/Music:** **Ayub Bachchu** (Musician), Novera Ahmed (Sculptor), Bibi Russell (Fashion).

**XI. Key Problems**

*   **Waterlogging** in Chittagong City.
*   Flooding (Southern areas).
*   Water supply issues (shortage, salinity) in Chittagong City.
*   Drug trafficking (border proximity).
*   Port congestion/delays.
*   **Climate Change Impacts on CHT Indigenous communities**.
