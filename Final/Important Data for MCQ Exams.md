### Important Data for MCQ Exams

**Tourism Industry:**
*   'Tour' origin: Latin 'tornus' (tool for making a circle).
*   Tourism definition timeframe: Min 24 hours, max 6 months (general leisure).
*   WTO (1993) definition: Max 1 consecutive year.
*   Incentive Tourism: $3 billion business in USA (at time of slide creation).
*   Cox's Bazar: World's longest natural sandy beach, **155 km**.
*   Sundarbans: World's largest mangrove forest, over **10,000 sq km**, UNESCO site.
    *   Royal Bengal Tigers: Estimated **over 1,000**.
    *   Bird species: **Over 350** recorded (slide 9), or **500+** (slide 8, likely total wildlife bird species).
*   Kuakata Beach: **320 km** S of Dhaka, **70 km** from Patuakhali HQ. Length **30 km**, width **6 km**.
*   St. Martin's Island: Area **8 km²**, **9 km** S of Cox's Bazar-Teknaf tip, **8 km** W of Myanmar.
*   Jaflong: Dawki Bridge built by British in **1932**.
*   Ratargul Forest: Only freshwater swamp forest in BD; underwater **5-6 months/year**. Kayak capacity: **3 people**.

**Archaeological Sites:**
*   Lalbagh Fort dimensions: **1082' x 800'**.
*   Ahsan Manjil construction: Begun **1859**, completed **1872**. Named after Khwaja Ahsanullah.
*   Sonargaon Museum: Established by Shilpacharya **Zainul Abedin**.
*   Shat Gambuj Mosque: **UNESCO World Heritage Site**. "Sixty Domed Mosque" but has **77 domes**, **60 stone pillars**.
*   Mainamati: **114 km** from Dhaka. Artifacts from **7th-13th century AD**.
*   Paharpur Buddha Vihara (Somapura Mahavihara): **UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985)**.
*   Mahasthangarh: Dates to at least **3rd century BC** (Brahmi inscription 1931). Used till **18th century AD**.
*   Kantajew Temple: Built **1752** by Maharaja Pran Nath. Originally **nava-ratna** (9 towers).
*   Wari-Bateshwar: Ancient city, **450 BC**. Discovered by **Hanif Pathan** (1930s). Excavation from **2000**. Possibly Ptolemy's "Sounagora".

**Culture and Society:**
*   Bangla literacy (older data): **50% men, 26% women**.
*   Flag: Green (countryside), Red (sun, **1971 war blood**). Anthem by **Rabindranath Tagore**.
*   National Parliament (Shongshad Bhabon): Designed by **Louis Kahn**.
*   Literature: Charyapadas (~1000 years old).
*   Pohela Falgun: Celebrated **February 13**.
*   Kite Flying Festival (Shakrain): **Jan 14 or 15** (end of Poush).
*   Independence Day: **March 26**.
*   International Mother Language Day: **February 21** (Language Movement **1952**).
*   Ekushey Book Fair: Entire month of **February**.
*   DITF: **Jan-Feb**.
*   Eid Ul Fitr: **1st day of Shawwal**.
*   Eid Ul Adha: **10th day of Dhu al-Hijjah**.
*   Buddha Purnima: Vaishakh full moon. Buddha's birth **623 BC**, enlightenment **588 BC**, Mahaparinirvana **543 BC**.
*   Lalon Shah: **1774–1890**.
*   Indigenous groups: ~**35** smaller groups, ~**2%** of population.
*   Chakma: Bizu festival (3 days, Chaitra).
*   Tripura language: **Kokborok**.
*   Santali language: **Austroasiatic/Munda** family.
*   Garo society: **Matrilineal**.

### Memory Techniques

1.  **Tourism Types (RCS-HCI):**
    *   **R**ecreational **C**hefs **S**erve **H**ot **C**urry **I**nstantly.
    *   (Recreational, Cultural, Sports, Health, Convention, Incentive)

2.  **Major UNESCO Sites (Sundar, Shat, Pahar):**
    *   "**Sun**ny **Shat**tered **Pahar** (mountain) is a UNESCO treasure."
    *   (Sundarbans, Shat Gambuj Mosque, Paharpur Buddha Vihara)

3.  **Key Archaeological Chronology (W-M-P-K):**
    *   "**W**ise **M**onks **P**reached **K**nowledge." (Roughly oldest to later prominent examples from list)
    *   (Wari-Bateshwar - 450BC, Mahasthangarh - 3rd C BC, Paharpur - 8th C AD, Kantajew - 18th C AD)

4.  **Bangla Language Forms (Sadhu vs. Cholit):**
    *   **Sadhu** = **S**erious/ **S**cholarly (formal).
    *   **Cholit** = **Ch**atty/ **C**ommon (vernacular).

5.  **Key Festivals & Dates:**
    *   Pohela **Boishakh** (Bangla New Year) - Easy to remember 1st Boishakh.
    *   Pohela **Falgun** (Spring) - Falgun is a spring month. Feb **13** (think Valentine's eve for spring love).
    *   **21** February (Intl. Mother Language Day) - The number **21** is iconic.
    *   **26** March (Independence Day) - Easy to remember as a fixed date.

6.  **Ethnic Groups & Locations (Simplified):**
    *   **C**hakma, **M**arma, **T**ripura = **C**HT **M**ain **T**ribes. (Chittagong Hill Tracts)
    *   **S**antal = **S**uperior numbers in **R**ajshahi/**R**angpur.
    *   **G**aro = **G**oes to **M**ymensingh.
    *   **M**onipuri = **M**ainly in **S**ylhet.

7.  **Folk Art Motifs (Symbolism):**
    *   **F**ish = **F**ertility.
    *   **L**otus = **L**ovely Purity.
    *   **S**heaf of Paddy = **S**uper Prosperity.

8.  **Lalbagh Fort Key Structures (MQ-BP-AH):**
    *   "**M**y **Q**ueen **B**ibi **P**ari needs an **A**udience **H**all."
    *   (Mosque, Bibi Pari's Tomb, Audience Hall)

9.  **Founders/Designers:**
    *   Sonargaon Museum: **Z**ainul **A**bedin (Think "ZA Museum").
    *   Parliament: Louis **Kahn** (Think "Kahn-structs Parliament").
    *   Wari-Bateshwar Discovery: Hanif **Pathan** (Think "Pathan found the Path").
