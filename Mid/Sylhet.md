**I. Overview & Nicknames**

*   **Nickname:** "Land of two leaves and a bud." Also known as the land of 360 Awlias (saints), especially Hazrat Shah Jalal (RA).
*   **Famous For:** Lush tea gardens, hills, diverse ethnic communities (Manipuri, Khasia), oranges, pineapples, natural gas, limestone.
*   **Key Industries:** Tea, cement, paper.
*   **Tea Hub:** Srimangal (Tea Capital, Bangladesh Tea Research Institute).
*   **Handicrafts:** Cane and bamboo products.

**II. Geography & Location**

*   **Location:** Northeastern Bangladesh, on the Surma River.
*   **Area:** ~12,635 sq km.
*   **Borders:**
    *   North: India (Meghalaya)
    *   South: India (Tripura)
    *   East: India (Assam)
    *   West: Netrokona & Kishoreganj districts (Mymensingh & Dhaka Divisions).

**III. Administration**

*   **Districts:** **4**
    *   **Mnemonic:** **SHMS** (**S**ylhet, **H**abiganj, **M**oulvibazar, **S**unamganj)
*   **Upazilas:** ~35-41
*   **City Corporation:** 1 (Sylhet City Corporation - est. 2001)
*   **Division Formed:** **1995** (separated from Chittagong Division).

**IV. History & Nomenclature**

*   **Name Origin:** Uncertain. Theories include Shilahat (Shila's market), Srihatta (Sati's bones 'Sri'+'Hadd'), Sil Hot Jah (Shah Jalal's command). Anglicized from Srihatta.
*   **Historical Names:** Jalalabad (Mughal), Shi-Li-Cha-T-L (Hiuen Tsang).
*   **Key Historical Points:**
    *   Ruled by Hindu/Buddhist kingdoms.
    *   **14th Century:** Arrival of Hazrat Shah Jalal, spread of Islam.
    *   British Era: Part of Assam Province.
    *   1947: Joined East Pakistan via referendum.
    *   1971: Became part of Bangladesh.
    *   1995: Became a separate Division.

**V. Demographics**

*   **Population:** ~11 million.
*   **Density:** ~873/sq km.
*   **Religion (Approx):** ~81% Muslim, ~13% Hindu.
*   **Ethnic Minorities (Tribes):** **Manipuri** & **Khasia** (largest groups), Garo, Patra, Tripura, etc.

**VI. Education**

*   **Key Universities:**
    *   **Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST):** Top-ranked public uni, focus on Sci/Tech, 1st in BD to use US credit system.
    *   **Sylhet Agricultural University (SAU).**
*   **Medical College:** Sylhet M.A.G. Osmani Medical College (Govt).
*   **Oldest College:** **Murari Chand College (MC College).**

**VII. Important Places & Tourist Spots**

*   **Tea Gardens:** **Srimangal** (Tea Capital), Lakkatura, **Malnichhara** (Oldest in subcontinent).
*   **Nature & Hills:**
    *   **Jaflong:** Hill station, stone collection, border area.
    *   **Lawachara National Park:** Protected forest, nature reserve.
    *   **Madhabkunda Waterfall:** High waterfall.
    *   **Bichnakandi:** Stone quarry, river, mountain views near border.
    *   **Shada Pathor (Bholaganj):** White stones, clear water.
*   **Water Bodies:**
    *   **Tanguar Haor:** Major wetland (Ramsar site), migratory birds.
    *   **Ratargul Swamp Forest:** ONLY freshwater swamp forest in Bangladesh.
    *   **Lalakhal:** River with colorful water, near Tamabil border.
    *   Other Haors (Hakaluki, Hail).
*   **Historical/Man-made:**
    *   **Keane Bridge (1936):** Landmark, "Gateway to Sylhet".
    *   **Ali Amjad Clock (1872):** Oldest clock tower in BD.
    *   Manipuri Rajbari (Palace).
    *   Hason Raja Museum (Sunamganj).
    *   Shrines of Hazrat Shah Jalal (R) & Shah Paran (R).

**VIII. Culture & Cuisine**

*   **Culture:** Blend of Bengali, Sufi, Hindu, Indigenous, and British influences.
*   **Festivals:** Manipuri Raash Leela, Urs of Shah Jalal, Hason Festival.
*   **Signature Food/Ingredients:**
    *   **Shatkora:** Unique local citrus fruit, used in curries (e.g., Beef Shatkora).
    *   **Seven-Layer Tea:** Famous in Srimangal.
    *   Akhni / Kisuri (Rice dishes).
    *   Shutki Shira (Dried fish curry).
    *   Handesh / Sunga Pitha (Traditional snacks/cakes).

**IX. Tribes / Ethnic Groups**

*   **Manipuri:** Known for distinct culture, dance (Raash Leela), weaving (every family has a loom).
*   **Khasi:** Matrilineal society, live in hills, known for betel leaf/nut cultivation. Associated with living root bridges.
*   **Jaintia (Synteng):** Related to Khasi, historically had a kingdom (Jaintiapur).

**X. Renowned Personalities**

*   **Hason Raja:** Mystic poet and songwriter.
*   **Shah Abdul Karim:** Baul singer ("Baul Samrat").
*   **General M.A.G. Osmani:** Commander-in-Chief of Liberation War (Medical College named after him).
*   Salman Shah: Film actor.
*   Kakon Bibi: Female freedom fighter (Bir Protik).
