**I. Human Society Basics**

*   **Definition:** Group living together in an organized way with shared customs, laws, organizations.
*   **Key Aspects:** Shared Culture, Social Structure, Institutions, Social Interaction, Change.
*   **Types (Giddens):** Hunting & Gathering, Agrarian, Pastoral, Traditional/Civilizations.

**II. Agrarian Society**

*   **Definition:** Economy relies on cultivating crops and farmland, often using plows/animals. Foundational stage for most civilizations.
*   **Key Characteristics:** *Mnemonic: **OL(U) C FHN PSR** (Occupational, Land(Unequal), Community, Family, Homogeneity, Nature, Primacy, Stratification, Religion)*
    *   **Occupational Structure:** Primarily farming + related crafts (weaving, pottery).
    *   **Land Ownership:** Unequal (Landlords, cultivators, sharecroppers, landless).
    *   **Less Specialization:** Roles based mainly on age/gender; homogeneous culture.
    *   **Community Centred:** Village life is central, strong traditions/customs.
    *   **Family Dominant:** Key social & economic unit; manages agriculture.
    *   **Nature Contact:** Close relationship with the natural environment.
    *   **Homogeneity:** Population similar despite caste/religion differences.
    *   **Primacy of Agriculture:** Economy revolves around farming.
    *   **Social Stratification:** Often based on caste linked to occupation (iron-smith, carpenter); barter system common.
    *   **Religion:** Becomes a separate institution with full-time officials (priests).

**III. Urbanization & Migration**

*   **Urbanization:** Shift from rural -> urban living; shift from agriculture -> industry/service jobs.
*   **Trend:** Globally >50% urban. Bangladesh urbanizing rapidly (from 7% in 1975 to >30% now). Dhaka = fast-growing megacity.
*   **Migration:** Temporary or permanent movement of people.
*   **Types of Migration:**
    *   Internal (within country) vs. External (International).
    *   Place-Based: Rural-Urban, Urban-Rural, Urban-Urban, Rural-Rural.
*   **Causes (Push-Pull Factors in Bangladesh):**
    *   **Push (from Rural):** Poverty, lack of jobs/facilities (health/edu), natural disasters, low income/living standard.
    *   **Pull (to Urban):** Better jobs/economy/lifestyle, better facilities (health/edu/transport), industry (RMG), safety.

**IV. Impact of Rural-Urban Migration/Urbanization in Bangladesh**

*   **Dual View:** Driver of economy vs. cause of urban problems & loss of rural life.
*   **Impacts:**

| Area        | Effects                                                                                                                                |
| :---------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Rural**   | Depopulation, potential unemployment, declining farm productivity (women take over), social issues (single families).                  |
| **Urban**   | Overcrowding, slums/squatter settlements, strain on facilities (water, sanitation, transport), crime, unplanned growth, pollution.      |
*   **Prospects/Positives:** Economic growth (GDP↑), improved infrastructure/services, social development (literacy, health), cultural exchange, women's empowerment.
*   **Problems/Negatives:** Slums/urban poverty (~21% urban poor), environmental degradation (pollution, loss of green space/wetlands), strain on utilities, traffic congestion, crime/insecurity, unplanned growth, weak policy (old laws, unimplemented policies).

**V. Local Governance Structure in Bangladesh**

*   **Two Tiers:** Rural & Urban.
*   **Rural (3-Tier System):**
    1.  **Union Parishad (UP):** Lowest level (4,573). Basic services, local markets.
    2.  **Upazila Parishad:** Middle level (495). Coordinates UPs, development projects, secondary services.
    3.  **Zilla Parishad:** Highest rural level (64 Districts). District-level planning, coordination.
*   **Urban (Single-Tier System):**
    1.  **Pourashava:** Municipalities for smaller towns (~331). Essential municipal services, town planning.
    2.  **City Corporation:** Major cities (12). Full range of municipal services, city planning, regulation.

**VI. Key Urban Challenges in Bangladesh (Focus on Dhaka)**

*   **Environmental Degradation:** Air, water, noise pollution; loss of green/wetlands.
*   **Strain on Services:** Inadequate utilities (water, electricity, sanitation, waste disposal).
*   **Traffic & Transport:** Severe congestion, pollution, inadequate planning & regulation.
*   **Urban Poverty & Slums:** Large population living in poor conditions.
*   **Governance:** Weak policy framework, need for better planning and management.
*   **Crime & Insecurity:** Common feature in large cities.
